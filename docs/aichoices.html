AI Choices
Scenario 1 of 7
Scenario-based AI literacy game

Build the skill.
Not just the submission.

A semester of realistic decisions about AI, learning, academic integrity, and career readiness. Your goal is not to use AI as little as possible. Your goal is to leave college more capable than when you entered it.

“AI helped me produce this” is not the same as “AI helped me become able to do this.”

Across six academic episodes and a final transfer challenge, you will build a profile across four dimensions:

Skill CapitalJudgmentIntegrity & OwnershipAI Readiness

Your choices can improve immediate performance while strengthening—or weakening—what you can do later without assistance.
