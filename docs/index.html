AI

Tools and resources for using generative AI in your teaching, and for understanding what the research says about it.

  • Redesign for Learning

    An interactive worksheet for redesigning assignments around academic integrity in the AI era. It walks through clarifying what students are learning, what they should do, what successful work looks like, where AI belongs, and what evidence makes learning visible — without turning the course into a surveillance system.
